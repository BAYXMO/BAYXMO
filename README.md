<> 🌍 About BAYXMO

**BAYXMO** is a long-term research and engineering initiative focused on developing an AI-powered physical educational companion robot.

The vision is to create a safe, intelligent, and compassionate physical companion that helps children learn, explore, communicate, and grow through personalized education and natural human-robot interaction.

BAYXMO brings together artificial intelligence, robotics, adaptive learning, computer vision, speech technologies, social robotics, and safety-centered design into one integrated physical platform.

---

<> 🎯 Vision

> To create a future where intelligent physical companions can make learning more personal, engaging, safe, and human.

<> 🛡️ Safety
Safety is a core design principle of BAYXMO.

The project aims to explore:

- Physical safety
- Safe motion and actuation
- Fault detection
- Power management
- Responsible AI behavior
- Privacy-aware system design
- Child-centered interaction
- Fail-safe mechanisms

Safety is not an additional feature of BAYXMO. It is part of the architecture.
